Stuff changed from original plan:
The major thing that I changed was having a stamina system. I removed the stamina
system because I believed that there wasn't much of a use for it. It would help if
there were other things such as fishing or foraging; since it is just farming, I
thought that it would've been good to just take it out.

Bugs/errors:
The hitboxes are weird, because it is tiled based, and certain objects do not fill up
the entire square, causing it to look off. As well, the hitboxes inside the house are
extra weird; I didn't have the time to add them properly, so you're able to walk through
the objects in the house. As well, there might be some empty parts on the map, as I
didn't really have the time to fully decorate everything.

Other:
-   If there is no button or way to get out of a state, click escape to leave
-   You can click the key "l" to grow the crops 1 day further
-   The data is only saved when a button is clicked on the end screen (next day or title)
-   The data folder can be deleted to restart from scratch again
-   The first number in general.txt represents the amount of money you have, and you can
    change it for more money
